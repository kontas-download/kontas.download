# Accounting consistency

New and edited sales, purchases, bank transactions and payrolls update GeneralEntry inside the same SQLite transaction through versioned triggers. Removing a source document removes its generated postings. Manual entries are preserved. Generated postings must be changed through their source document.

The account mapping follows this project's existing import scripts: sales 2410/5001 and 2410/4492; purchases 6118/4430 and 2444/4430; incoming bank /2410, outgoing 4430/bank. Payroll uses 6304 against 4480, 4481 and 4482, including employer contributions. The payroll date is the last calendar day of the month. These defaults are application conventions, not automatic classification of every economic transaction.

Invoice arithmetic uses fixed-point BigInt values: quantity and unit price up to six decimals, tax rate up to four decimals, line net and tax rounded half away from zero to two decimals. Document totals sum rounded lines. SQLite stores existing REAL columns for compatibility; a future integer-cents schema migration must separately reconcile historical values.

Historical entries are not silently rewritten or backfilled. Review unmatched historical documents before reposting them. The migration adds triggers and indexes only, and runs once per database.

Run npm test for authentication, company isolation, rounding, validation, posting lifecycle and transaction rollback regression tests.

A purchase may name its own debit account (`Purchase.debitAccount`, for example an inventory or fixed-asset account from the chart). When empty the default 6118 is used, so historical purchases and journal rows are unchanged. Payable/VAT accounts (44xx, 2410, 2444) cannot be chosen, and the account must exist in the chart of accounts. Capitalisation rules, depreciation and cost of goods sold are not automated.

Credit notes: `POST /api/invoices/{id}/credit` creates a KS document (`docType='KS'`, `originalSaleId`, `correctionReason`) as a negative sale. Lines are entered as positive amounts; stored sums and the generated journal rows (same accounts as the sale) are negative, so revenue, VAT and receivables net out. The total credited per invoice cannot exceed the original net and gross amounts, the tax mode is taken from the original invoice, and the PDF is titled "KREDITINĖ ..." with a reference to the original. An invoice with credit notes, and any credit note, cannot be edited; delete credit notes first. Debit notes (DS) and purchase-side corrections are not implemented. i.SAF export refuses credit notes until their amount signs are verified against specification 1.2.1.

Invoice archive: every successfully emailed invoice stores the exact PDF bytes that were attached, its SHA-256, recipient, message id and a JSON snapshot of the document (seller and buyer details at that moment) in `InvoiceArchive`. Rows are append-only (triggers reject UPDATE and DELETE), a repeated request id does not create a second row, and the copy stays available after the sale is deleted. `GET /api/invoices/{id}/archive` lists copies and `?version=<id>` downloads one after re-checking its hash. The database file therefore grows by the size of each sent PDF; archive backups separately from the encryption key.

Bank statements and payments: `POST /api/bank/import` parses a CSV statement (delimiter and column names are auto-detected; Lithuanian, English and Russian headers; single amount column or debit/credit pair). Without `commit` it is a dry run. Any invalid row rejects the whole file, so nothing is imported partially. Every row gets a hash of company, date, amount, counterparty, details and reference (plus an occurrence counter for identical rows within one file), so importing the same file again adds nothing. Imported rows use the existing bank journal triggers (2710 against 2410 or 4430) and respect closed periods.

`PaymentAllocation` links a bank transaction to a sale (incoming money) or a purchase (outgoing money), with partial payments. Database triggers enforce company scope, direction, that allocations never exceed the bank amount or the document balance (a sale's balance is its total plus its credit notes), and that credit notes cannot be paid. Allocations are immutable (delete and recreate); invoices with allocations cannot be deleted. `GET /api/bank/matches` suggests matches: "high" needs both the invoice number in the payment details and the exact remaining balance; amount alone is never enough. Suggestions are applied only on an explicit request (`POST /api/bank/matches` applies the high-confidence ones). The invoice status text is still manual; paid and open amounts come from `GET /api/bank/allocations`.

Receivables and reminders: `GET /api/receivables?asOf=` lists open sales (balance = total + credit notes - allocated bank payments) with days overdue and ageing buckets (not due, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+); a sale without a due date ages from its invoice date. `GET /api/invoices/{id}/reminder` returns an editable Lithuanian draft; nothing is sent until `POST` with a request id. The balance is re-checked at send time, paid invoices and credit notes are refused, the same invoice cannot be reminded again within 3 days (including after an unconfirmed delivery), and a repeated request id never sends twice. The invoice PDF is attached and the company's own SMTP settings are used. Each attempt is recorded in the append-only `ReminderLog`. There is no automatic or scheduled sending: every reminder is a manual action.

Tax calendar: `GET /api/tax-calendar` lists due dates from the company's tax profile. FR0600 applies automatically to STANDARD_VAT companies (period month, quarter or half-year; due on the 25th of the month after the period, per VMI KM1112); FR0564 (25th), GPM313 (15th of the next month), GPM312 (15 February) and PLN204 (15 June) are off until enabled for the company because applicability depends on facts the application does not know. Dates are nominal. The carry-over of a deadline that falls on a weekend or a Lithuanian public holiday (fixed holidays, Easter Sunday and Monday, All Souls' Day) is computed and shown separately, but the legal rule itself has not been verified against a source, so the earlier date should be used. Periods can be marked as filed; nothing is submitted or sent from the calendar. i.SAF register deadlines are not included because they have not been verified.

Recurring invoices: a template (customer, series, frequency month/quarter/year, next date, optional end date, payment days, lines) never issues anything by itself. When the next date has come, `POST /api/recurring/{id}/issue` creates an ordinary sales invoice through the same code path as the invoice API (tax mode of the issue date, posting, numbering, closed-period and duplicate-number checks), with the next number of the series, status "Neapmokėta" and a due date of invoice date plus payment days. Each period can be issued once (`RecurringRun` primary key); the next date keeps the original day of month (31 Jan, 28 Feb, 31 Mar). A failed issue (closed period, VAT rate that does not match the company's tax mode, duplicate number) changes nothing and does not advance the template. Deleting a template keeps the invoices it issued; deleting an issued invoice forgets that period's run record. Sending the issued invoice by email is a separate manual step.

Supplier e-invoice import: `POST /api/purchases/import-xml` reads a UBL 2.1 / Peppol BIS invoice (type 380) or credit note (`CreditNote`, or `Invoice` with type 381) in EUR. Without `commit` it is a preview. The reader refuses anything it would have to guess: documents with DOCTYPE/ENTITY, other document types, other currencies, document- or line-level discounts and charges, VAT categories other than standard, zero and exempt (for example reverse charge), line sums that do not add up to the invoice total, and a VAT total that differs from the sum of per-line VAT recalculated with the application's rounding (such invoices must be entered by hand). The buyer's VAT or company code must match the active company. A file is imported once per company (SHA-256), and the same supplier invoice number is not imported twice. An unknown supplier is created only when the user confirms it. The purchase goes through the same code path as manual entry, so the VAT deduction follows the company's tax mode (normally 100 % for a VAT payer); the user should review the right to deduct afterwards. The original XML is stored unchanged in the append-only `PurchaseSource` table. PDF invoices are not read automatically.

Supplier credit note import (same endpoint, type 381): the file becomes a supplier credit note (KS) against an existing purchase of the same company and the same supplier, through the same shared code as `POST /api/purchases/{id}/credit` (`src/lib/purchase-credit.ts`: date not before the original, credited net/VAT/gross and VAT rates not above the original, cumulative deductible-VAT reversal, ledger rows from the existing database triggers). The original is found through `BillingReference/InvoiceDocumentReference/ID` (number, or series+number) and, when present, its `IssueDate`; exactly one `BillingReference` is accepted. The file is refused when the original is not found, the date differs, the original belongs to another supplier, several purchases match, the supplier is unknown, the currency is not EUR, lines do not add up, or the per-line VAT recalculation differs. Without a reference the preview lists the purchases of the same supplier and the user must pick one explicitly (`originalPurchaseId`); the server re-checks every rule and never picks one itself. The preview (no `commit`) returns `credit.original` and `credit.effect` (net, VAT, gross, reversed deductible VAT). The SHA-256 once-per-company rule, the no-duplicate supplier number rule and the append-only `PurchaseSource` storage apply as for invoices; no supplier is created for a credit note.

Rule clarifications:
- Open receivables and payment suggestions skip sales whose status text is "Apmokėta" (paid) unless money is already linked to them, so historical invoices do not appear as debts when payment tracking starts; mark a new invoice as unpaid ("Neapmokėta") to track it. Purchases have no status and are all treated as open.
- An invoice number in payment details matches only as a whole token ("SF 12" never matches "SF 123"); leading zeros and separators are ignored; a number made only of digits needs at least 5 digits unless the series is given; a long number alone is accepted without the series. If two documents fit equally well the suggestion is "medium" and says so. Within one pass a document's balance is reduced by earlier suggestions, and applying the "high" suggestions skips rows that no longer fit instead of failing the batch.
- Database triggers refuse to lower a sale or purchase total, or to issue a credit note, below the money already linked to it.
- Deleting a company removes its payments, recurring templates and credit notes first; the append-only evidence tables (invoice archive, supplier XML, reminder log) are intentionally kept.
- Audit triggers for sales, purchases and bank transactions are rebuilt by a migration so columns added later (debit account, credit-note links, import keys) are recorded.
- Bank CSV: a D/K (debit/credit) column sets the sign of the amount; rows that look like opening/closing balances or turnover reject the file; a file whose amounts are all positive and has no direction column needs explicit confirmation; the duplicate key includes the account name, so the same file imported into another account is new.
- "Today" for due dates, the tax calendar and recurring invoices is the date in Europe/Vilnius, not in UTC. A recurring template cannot be moved back to a period that was already issued. A reminder re-checks the balance and the 3-day window after rendering the PDF, just before reserving the send.
- FR0600 lists documents without lines for manual handling; the i.SAF export refuses taxed purchases whose supplier has no VAT code and explains credit notes correctly. A purchase debit account cannot be a cash/bank (27xx) or income (5xxx) account.

Further rule clarifications (changes to the rules above):
- The "paid" status rule applies only to invoices created before payment tracking was introduced (before the `2026-10-bank-payments-v1` migration time). An invoice created afterwards is tracked from its bank links regardless of the status text, because the invoice form saves every new invoice as "Apmokėta".
- The guards against lowering a sale/purchase total below linked payments live in their own migration (`2026-10-payment-guards-v1`) so they reach the live database. A credit note on a fully paid invoice is allowed; the resulting overpayment is listed on the receivables screen and in `GET /api/receivables` (`overpaid`).
- The bank duplicate key no longer includes the account label (a free-text field), so re-importing a file under another label adds nothing; two identical genuine rows in separate files are still treated as duplicates and shown in the preview count. A "Type"/"Tipas" column is no longer taken for a debit/credit indicator, and only real balance/turnover rows (for example "Pradinis likutis", "Closing balance"), not names starting with "Total" or "Bendra", reject a file.
- A document number made only of letters or zeros never matches, and a bare long number does not match an amount such as "12345.00".
- FR0600 now lists documents whose header totals differ from the sum of their lines for manual handling.

Bank statements in the standard format: `POST /api/bank/import` also accepts ISO 20022 camt.053 XML (the standard export of Lithuanian banks) alongside CSV; the format is detected from the content. The reader takes the direction of each entry from `CdtDbtInd`, the account from the IBAN (stored as the bank account name), the counterparty from the opposite party of the entry and the payment details from the remittance information, and it only uses entries the bank has booked (pending entries are skipped and reported). A file is refused when the currency is not EUR, the account or a date is missing, the XML has a DOCTYPE, or when the opening balance plus the booked entries does not equal the closing balance, so a truncated or edited statement cannot be imported. The duplicate key is the bank's own entry reference together with the IBAN (content-based only when the bank gives no reference), so an overlapping later export adds only the new entries. CSV import is unchanged. MT940 is not supported.

Credit notes in i.SAF: per the VMI import checks (`isaf11123.pdf`, kept in `docs/vmi`), a negative taxable value is allowed only in a credit or debit invoice, and a corrected invoice must be referenced. A sales credit note is exported as type `KS` with negative `TaxableValue` and `Amount` and a `Reference` to the original invoice number and date; a credit note whose amounts are not all negative, without an original, or without VAT (unless its lines carry an explicit 0 % code) is refused. Purchase credit notes (supplier KS) are exported the same way, as type `KS` with negative amounts and a reference to the original document (see "Supplier credit notes" below). The export also refuses a document whose header totals differ from the sum of its lines.

Payments outside the bank: a sale or purchase can be marked as settled in cash or by offset (`POST /api/bank/settlements`, button "Apmokėta kitaip" on the receivables screen). It counts as paid like a bank link, the sum of bank links and settlements is capped by the document balance, totals cannot be lowered below it, and it can be deleted to reopen the balance. Sales imported from JSON keep the paid status of the source system (they are treated as history, not as tracked invoices).

An add-on whose migration fails is logged and skipped; the product keeps working.

Supplier credit notes: `POST /api/purchases/{id}/credit` (button "KS" in the purchases list) records a credit note received from a supplier as a negative purchase linked to the original (`docType='KS'`), exactly like sales credit notes. Lines are entered positive and stored negative; the number is the supplier's credit note number. The VAT deduction follows the original: the same percentage is reversed (a purchase whose deduction was never reviewed stays unreviewed), and for a buyer without VAT deduction the supplier VAT stays in the expense. The journal rows are negative on the same accounts as the purchase. The total credited cannot exceed the original; the credit date cannot be earlier than the original; the original and its credit notes cannot be edited and the original cannot be deleted while credit notes exist. The payable balance is the purchase total plus its credit notes; credit notes cannot be paid, a credit note issued after payment shows as an overpayment (refund due from the supplier). FR0600 reduces fields 25 and 35 by the credit note, and the i.SAF purchase register exports it as type `KS` with negative amounts and a reference to the original document. XML import of supplier credit notes (UBL type 381) is described under the supplier e-invoice import. Debit notes are not implemented.
- Credit notes (sales and purchases) may only use VAT rates present on the original document (legacy lines: the rate implied by their amounts), and their VAT may not exceed the original VAT. Reversed purchase deductions never exceed the deduction originally taken; the last credit takes the exact remainder.
- Manual offsets refuse only the document's own settlement account (2410 for sales, 4430 for purchases). Bank import overlap warnings ignore rows already matched by hash; camt content keys exclude NtryRef.
- Nothing is submitted to VMI automatically: i.SAF and FR0600 are prepared files that the user uploads manually.

Data export, import and history:
- `GET /api/companies/{id}/history?from=&to=&entity=&limit=&offset=&format=json|csv` lists the company's `AccountingAudit` rows (administrators only, like the accounting control). Each event has a short readable summary: `+ …` created, `− …` deleted, `field: old → new` for changes. Migration `2026-10-audit-payments-v1` adds audit triggers for payment links (`PaymentAllocation`) and settlements outside the bank (`ManualSettlement`); both tables are immutable, so INSERT and DELETE are recorded. Earlier migrations are unchanged.
- `GET /api/companies/{id}/export` downloads a package `{format:'kontas-company', version:1, exportedAt, appVersion, checksum, data}`; the checksum is the SHA-256 of the data with keys sorted. It contains the company record, tax periods and obligations, the shared chart of accounts, clients, stock, sales and purchases with lines (credit notes with their originals), bank transactions, payment links, settlements, payrolls, the journal, recurring templates, delivery/reminder logs (recipient and status only), the accounting lock and the change history. It never contains SMTP settings, users, sessions or login attempts. The PDF invoice archive and supplier XML originals are not included; they stay in the source database and its backups.
- `POST /api/companies/import-package` (body: the package; `?commit=1` writes, otherwise a preview) always creates a NEW company; nothing is written into an existing one. (`/api/companies/import` remains the older legacy-JSON importer.) The format, version, checksum, size (50 MB), field names and types and every link inside the package are checked. Policy for duplicates: the new company's name must differ from every existing company (`?name=` renames it); if the company code or VAT code already exists, the import is refused unless `?allowDuplicateCode=1` explicitly confirms a copy. The preview runs the complete import inside the transaction and rolls it back, so every database rule is applied exactly as in the real import.
- Import order and triggers: all ids are remapped. Externally sourced `externalId` values are globally unique and are cleared in the copy. Payment links and settlements are inserted before credit notes, because the database caps payments by the balance (total plus credit notes), and a credit note issued after a payment would otherwise make a valid historical payment look too large. The posting triggers fire while documents are inserted; then the journal of the new company is replaced, inside the same transaction, by the source journal row for row with `sourceId` pointing to the copy's own documents (sales, purchases, bank, payroll, settlements) and the counts and amount totals are verified. Historical postings (manual rows, legacy imports, rows posted under older rules) are thus identical; the preview warns when they differ from what today's rules would generate. The audit rows written by the triggers during the import are replaced by the source history (record ids remapped, JSON snapshots keep the source values) plus one `IMPORT` record. The accounting lock is restored last, so the closed period is protected again in the copy. Historical tax status (`sellerVatMode`, `sellerIsVatPayer`, `buyerVatMode`, deduction) and `createdAt` are kept. Chart-of-accounts codes that do not exist are added; existing account names are never changed. Duplicate document numbers inside a package are refused by the existing number trigger.
- `GET /api/export/{clients|stock|sales|purchases|journal|bank}?format=csv|json` exports one section of the active company (or `?companyId=`). CSV is UTF-8 with BOM, `;` delimiter and RFC 4180 quoting; text cells starting with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, tab or CR get a leading apostrophe (CSV injection). JSON sales and purchases include their lines.
- `POST /api/import/clients` and `/api/import/stock` (body `{csv, commit}`) import a CSV with the export's column names (`name` required). Preview first; any invalid row rejects the file. Existing rows are never changed: a client whose code or VAT code (or, without both, name) already exists, or a stock item with the same code or barcode (or name in the same warehouse), is skipped and reported, as are repeats within the file. Bank statements and supplier invoices keep their own importers.
- `GET /api/admin/backup` (administrators) downloads a consistent copy of the whole database made with SQLite's online backup API into a temporary file that is deleted after the download. It contains every company, password hashes and encrypted SMTP passwords, but not the encryption key; store it accordingly. There is no restore endpoint. Restore procedure (operator): stop the application (`docker stop kontas-app`), keep the current `prisma/dev.db` and its `-wal`/`-shm` files aside, put the downloaded file in place as `dev.db` without `-wal`/`-shm`, check `PRAGMA integrity_check`, start the application and verify the companies; the same encryption key must be configured for SMTP passwords to work.
- The UI is `src/components/CompanyDataPanel.tsx` (props `companyId`, `companyName`).

Languages: the interface is available in Lithuanian, Russian and English and the user can switch at any time (cookie `lang`, default from Accept-Language, otherwise Lithuanian). Texts live in `src/lib/i18n/messages/<namespace>.ts`; `tests/i18n.cjs` requires identical keys and placeholders in all three languages. Server error messages from the API (`apiError`, access and CSRF checks) are translated into Russian or English according to the `lang` cookie (`translateServerError` in `src/lib/errors.ts`).

Document language: customer-facing documents (invoice PDF, invoice email, payment reminder) are issued in the customer's language (`Client.documentLang`: lt, ru or en; empty = Lithuanian) or in the language chosen for that document (`?lang=` on the PDF, email draft and reminder draft endpoints; `lang` in the send request, so the attached PDF and the text always match). It is independent of the interface language. The language used is stored in the archived invoice snapshot. Labels, titles, number formats, notes about non-PVM sellers and the reverse-charge notice (when a line carries `PVM15`/`PVM34`/`PVM19` or `PVM25`–`PVM27`) are translated; the legal content (VAT status, amounts, dates in ISO form) is unchanged. The language travels with the customer in company export packages.

MT940: `POST /api/bank/import` also reads SWIFT MT940 statements (format detected from the content: `:20:`, `:25:`, `:60F:`, `:61:`, `:86:`, `:62F:`; a SWIFT envelope is tolerated). Direction comes from the D/C mark of every entry (RC/RD reversals are handled), the account from `:25:`, the counterparty and purpose from `:86:` (structured `?20..?33` or `/NAME/../REMI/..`). Only EUR accounts are accepted, every statement must reconcile (opening + entries = closing), and the duplicate key is the bank reference after `//` together with the account, otherwise the usual content key. Entries without a year in the booking date take the year closest to the value date.

i.SAF tax codes of lines: the accountant can assign an i.SAF tax code to any sale or purchase line after the fact (`TaxLineCode`, migration `2026-10-tax-codes-v1`, primary key kind + line id); the issued document and its PDF are never changed. Triggers enforce that the line belongs to the company and that the code is allowed for sales/purchases; every insert, change and removal is recorded in `AccountingAudit` (entities `SaleTaxCode` / `PurchaseTaxCode`, record id = line id, actor set by the API). Removing a line removes its code. Codes follow the accounting lock of their document: setting, changing or clearing the code of a line whose document date is on or before `lockedThrough` is refused like any other change in a closed period (migration `2026-10-tax-codes-v2`). `GET /api/tax-reports/line-codes?start=&end=` lists lines without an automatic code or with an explicit one; `PUT` (`{items:[{kind,itemId,taxCode|null,note}]}`) sets or clears codes (administrators and accountants, same-origin, active company only). UI: `src/components/TaxCodesPanel.tsx` on the FR0600 tab.
- Editing a document (`PUT /api/invoices/{id}`, `PUT /api/purchases/{id}`) replaces its lines; the codes are carried over to the new line at the same position with the same name, VAT rate and amounts, otherwise to any unused new line with the same name, rate and amounts. A code that fits no new line is returned as `droppedTaxCodes` (shown to the user after saving), written to the history (`SaleTaxCodeReview` / `PurchaseTaxCodeReview`) and the document is marked in `TaxCodeReview`: the i.SAF export and the FR0600 draft refuse it until the accountant assigns codes again or confirms the document (`PUT ... {confirm:[{kind,docId}]}`, button in the panel). Changing any code of the document also clears the mark. The mark travels in company packages and is removed with the document.
- Credit notes (sales and supplier, manual or imported from XML) take over the original's code for each line when the original lines with the same VAT rate (and the same name, if any) share one code; lines where this is ambiguous are returned as `missingTaxCodes` and shown to the user.
- The catalogue `src/lib/tax-codes.ts` (VMI table "FR0600 + i.SAF kodas", i.SAF 1.2.1 description, import checks) gives per code and side: TaxPercentage (empty when the classifier has no rate, `0` for 0 %), the VAT mode (charged / zero / self-assessed / margin), the allowed counterparty, FR0600 base and VAT fields and FR0564 field. An explicit code wins; lines without one keep the automatic domestic classification, and files without explicit codes are byte-identical to before.
- Exported to i.SAF: PVM1, PVM2, PVM3, PVM58, PVM5, PVM12, PVM13 (EU VAT customer only), PVM14, PVM15, PVM34, PVM16–18, PVM20, PVM21, PVM25–27 (purchases), PVM43–45, PVM60. Refused with an issue because the VMI documents do not fix their i.SAF data: PVM25 on sales, PVM19, margin codes PVM32/33/52/53, PVM9, private use PVM6–8/PVM59. `EXCLUDED` (purchases, e.g. import with VAT paid to customs; PVM48 is never produced) leaves the document out of i.SAF; a document mixing excluded and other lines is refused.
- Self-assessed VAT (PVM16–18, 20, 21, 25–27, 43–45, 60): the supplier document must carry no VAT; Amount = net × code rate per document and code with the invoice rounding (`vatOnNetCents`). Counterparties: EU VAT code → Country empty; no VAT code or a non-EU code → Country must be an ISO alpha-2 code in the client's country field. Credit notes keep the negative-sign rule for every code; lines with net 0 are refused.
- FR0600 applies the same counterparty rules as the i.SAF export (shared in `tax-codes.ts`): a document whose explicit code does not fit the counterparty (for example PVM13 or PVM12 for a Lithuanian customer, PVM20 from an EU VAT payer) is listed for manual handling and not summed. A purchase without codes from a foreign supplier without a VAT code is listed for manual handling. An explicitly coded ordinary purchase (PVM1/2/3/58) whose rate does not fit the code is not added to fields 25 and 35.
- FR0600: `fields` keeps its eight fields; 36 = 29 + 29A + 30 + 31 − 35 while fields 32–34 are zero. When VAT calculated by the buyer is present (32–34 ≠ 0) `f36` is `null` with a note and the panel says that EDS computes it: the formula of field 36 is not stated in the VMI sources kept in `docs/vmi` (KM1739 only says it is computed automatically), so no number is guessed; `extended` holds 12–24 and 32–34 from coded lines, `computed` lists self-assessed VAT, `fr0564` (13, 14, 18 upper bound) and `notes` are information only. The deduction of self-assessed VAT and PVM9 is listed as unreviewed (not in 35); margin codes and the 23/24 split of PVM43–45 are listed for manual handling. The final payable amount and fields 26–28 are completed in EDS; nothing is submitted automatically.
- Company packages include `taxLineCodes` with line ids remapped; their history is remapped like other records.

Users and roles (migration `2026-10-users-v1`, `src/lib/user-schema.ts`, `src/lib/users.ts`): several people use one installation; there is no self-registration, the first administrator comes from the first-start bootstrap and administrators create the other accounts on `/users`.
- Roles: `admin` can do everything. `accountant` does all normal accounting work in every company (documents, clients, stock, bank, payroll, ledger, tax reports and i.SAF codes, exports of sections, switching the active company, editing existing company details and SMTP). Administrator-only (`requireAdminApi` / `requireAdminPage` in `src/lib/access.ts`): `/api/users*` and `/users`, `GET /api/admin/backup`, `GET /api/companies/{id}/export`, `POST /api/companies/import-package`, `POST /api/companies/import` (legacy JSON), `GET /api/companies/{id}/history`, `GET|PUT /api/companies/{id}/accounting-control` (closing periods), `POST /api/companies` and `DELETE /api/companies/{id}`. `requireApiAccess` / `requirePageAccess` accept both roles; any other role value is refused. The UI hides these actions from accountants; the API is the guard.
- `User.disabledAt` (INTEGER, NULL = active): a disabled user cannot sign in (same generic error as a wrong password) and `verifySessionToken` ignores their sessions; disabling also deletes their sessions. Triggers `user_role_INSERT` / `user_role_UPDATE` allow only `admin` and `accountant`; the unique index `idx_user_email_nocase` on `LOWER(email)` makes e-mails unique ignoring case (skipped with a log message only if existing rows already collide). The last active administrator cannot be disabled, demoted or deleted (HTTP 409).
- `GET|POST /api/users`, `PATCH|DELETE /api/users/{id}` (administrators, same-origin): create with `{email,name,role,password}` (at least 10 characters) or `generatePassword:true`; PATCH changes `name`, `role`, `disabled` and/or resets the password (given or generated). A generated password is returned once in the response and never stored in plain text; responses never contain `passwordHash`. A reset ends the user's sessions (except the administrator's own current one) and clears their `LoginAttempt` row. DELETE removes a user who has never acted (no `AccountingAudit.actorId`, no changes to other users); otherwise the user is disabled and the response says `deleted:false`, so the history keeps its author.
- `POST /api/auth/password` (`{currentPassword,newPassword}`): any signed-in user changes their own password; a wrong current password counts towards the sign-in throttle of that e-mail; all other sessions of the user end.
- `UserLog` is an append-only log (UPDATE/DELETE refused by triggers): actor, user id and e-mail, action (`CREATE`, `RENAME`, `ROLE`, `DISABLE`, `ENABLE`, `PASSWORD_RESET`, `PASSWORD_CHANGE`, `DELETE`, `LOGIN`), details without passwords or hashes. The latest entry is shown as "last action" on `/users`.

## Demonstration company

- `POST /api/demo` (administrators only, same-origin; empty body or `{name}`) creates a new company with entirely fictional data in one transaction and returns `{companyId,name}`. - The data is written with plain SQL inserts, so the same triggers apply as for documents entered by hand: ledger postings, the duplicate-number ban, payment and credit-note guards, closed-period and audit triggers. Payments are linked with `allocatePayment`, the cash payment with `settleManually`, templates are checked by `parseTemplate`. Only accounts of the public chart of accounts are used (2410, 272, 2710, 2444, 4430, 4480–4482, 4492, 5001, 6118, 6304). Salaries are not paid through the bank: the outgoing bank trigger debits 4430, not 4480, so the payroll liabilities stay unpaid.

## Rules registry

- Values that depend on legislation are kept in the registry `src/lib/rules` (`vat.ts`, `payroll.ts`, `calendar.ts`, `isaf.ts`), not in the calculation code. Every entry has `id`, `value`, validity dates `effectiveFrom`/`effectiveTo` (inclusive), a `basis` (act title, article, source id from `src/lib/help/sources.ts`), a status, `verifiedOn`, `verifiedBy`, `reviewBy` and `example` (title of the control test in `tests/*.cjs`). When a law changes, a new entry is added and the old one is closed; nothing is deleted; changes are recorded in `src/lib/rules/CHANGELOG.md`.
- Access only through `rules.at(id, date)` (Vilnius calendar date; a clear error when no entry is valid). The registry is used by `domesticTaxCode` (i.SAF and FR0600: 21, 9, 5%, 12% from 2026-01-01), `monthlyDueDate` and the tax calendar (deadlines), and by the payroll form suggestion (`payrollRules` from the server page). Dates before 2016-10-01 (start of i.SAF) are treated as 2016-10-01, so the earlier behaviour is unchanged. The i.SAF code rates stay in the catalogue `src/lib/tax-codes.ts` (entry `isaf.taxCodes.catalogue`). The test `tests/rules.cjs` forbids hard-coded rates outside the registry.
- Statuses: `verified` – checked against the stated official source; `provisional` – temporary. **The accountant must check provisional rules before the results are used in returns.** In the initial registry the VAT rates and the i.SAF catalogue are verified (VMI table FR0600 + i.SAF kodas); deadlines and payroll rates are provisional.
- Freshness: `TAX_RULES_AS_OF` and `rulesFreshness(today)` (`src/lib/rules/index.ts`); the overview page shows "Tax rules up to date as of …" and a warning when the `reviewBy` of an entry in force has passed or the newest check is older than 400 days. The `/rules` page (administrators and accountants) only lists all rules.
- Audit: `npm run rules:audit` (`scripts/rules-audit.cjs`) prints every entry with flags (passed `reviewBy`, provisional, verified more than 180 days ago) and exits with 1 when a review date has passed (`-- --warn-only` – always 0).
- Payroll calculation (`/payroll`, `src/lib/payroll-runs.ts`, `payroll-calc.ts`): employees and monthly runs; NPD, income tax (GPM) and Sodra contributions come from the rules registry for the date of the month. A line keeps the computed and the final value; a manual change requires a reason and is written to `PayrollLog`. The calculation is **provisional**: without verification by the accountant (the "verified" mark) it must not be used for declarations. Posting creates the existing `Payroll`/`PayrollEmployee` document, so the existing triggers create the payroll journal entries.
- Fixed assets (`/assets`, `src/lib/fixed-assets.ts`): the asset register, monthly depreciation (straight-line or declining balance; the default method is a company setting), disposal and its cancellation. Posting a month creates `FixedAssetPosting` rows; only the latest month can be undone. Changing the method, useful life, residual value or factor requires a reason and is written to `FixedAssetChange`. The capitalization threshold, tax lives and factor come from the rules registry (provisional - the accountant must verify them).
- Inventory costing (`/stock`, `src/lib/inventory.ts`): off by default (`OFF`); a company can choose weighted average or FIFO. Purchases and sales write immutable `InventoryMovement` rows (FIFO layers in `InventoryLayer`) and cost journal entries; posted documents and stock cards are protected, insufficient stock blocks a sale and the method cannot be changed once movements exist. A manual quantity or value adjustment (`/api/inventory/adjustments`) requires a reason and creates a new movement.
- FR0564 (`/fr0564`, `GET /api/tax-reports/fr0564`, `src/lib/fr0564.ts`) is a draft only: the month's sales to buyers with a VAT code of another EU state, grouped by VAT code; the field follows the i.SAF code of the line (PVM13 → 13, PVM19 → 14, PVM15 / PVM34 → 18, the last conditional), credit notes reduce the amounts, amounts are rounded to whole euro. Lines without an i.SAF code, an EU buyer without a VAT code and documents without lines or with mismatching totals are listed for manual handling. Not filled: appendices FR0564R / FR0564T / FR0564TP, advances, item 4.2 transfers, VAT code validation. The due date is the rules registry entry `calendar.fr0564.dueDay` (provisional). Source: VMI order VA-34 as amended by VA-40 (from 2025-05-01).
- GPM313 (`/gpm313`, `GET /api/tax-reports/gpm313`, `src/lib/gpm313.ts`) is a draft only: the monthly gross (field 5), the NPD applied (information only) and the GPM withheld (sum of fields 6 + 7; final values with manual overrides) from posted payroll calculations (older Payroll records for months without a calculation). When a payout date is set on the payroll calculation (`PayrollRun.payoutDate`, migration `2026-10-payroll-payout-v1`), the withheld GPM is split into field 6 (up to and including the 15th) or field 7 (after the 15th); without a payout date fields 6 and 7 are not split and field 5 is shown by the accrual month. Fields 8–12 and tax-exempt or foreign-taxed payments are not filled. The due date is the 15th of the next month, rules registry entry `calendar.gpm313.dueDay` (provisional). Source: VMI order VA-121 as amended by VA-105 (2025-11-20).
- GPM312 (`/gpm312`, `GET /api/tax-reports/gpm312`, `src/lib/gpm312.ts`) – draft only: posted (POSTED) payroll calculations of the year (older Payroll records where there is no calculation) are summed per employee: appendix GPM312L L8 (gross, NPD/GPM/contributions not deducted), L10 (GPM withheld), code 01, personal code from the employee card (a missing one is flagged), sums for declaration fields 4 and 5. Not filled: other income types (03, 05, 08, etc.), allocation of payments to a year by payment date, other class A and B payments. The due date is 15 February of the next year, rules registry entry `calendar.gpm312.due` (provisional). Source: VMI order VA-9 (as amended by VA-24 of 2026-03-26).
- PLN204 (`/pln204`, `GET /api/tax-reports/pln204`, `src/lib/pln204.ts`) – draft and `.ffdata` export of the annual corporate income tax return: computes revenue (field 18) and accounting profit before tax (field 19) from the General Ledger, supports manual adjustments for fields 20 (PLN204S non-deductible expenses), 26 (PLN204Z non-taxable income) and 45 (carried-forward losses capped at 70% for the standard rate) and selection of the rate code (`1` standard 15%/16%/17%, `2` reduced 5%/6%/7%, `3` first-year 0%). The due date is 15 June of the next year (`calendar.pln204.due`). Drafts of all five forms (`FR0600`, `FR0564`, `GPM313`, `GPM312`, `PLN204`) can be downloaded in VMI EDS `.ffdata` XML format (`src/lib/ffdata.ts`: exact `FormDefId`, page and field names from the official `.mxfd`/`.ffdata` templates, manual fields 26–28 in `FR0600` and appendices `FR0564P`/`R`/`T`/`TP`, `GPM312L`/`GPM312U`, `PLN204S`/`PLN204Z`).
